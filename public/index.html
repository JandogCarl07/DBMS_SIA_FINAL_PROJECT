How V-RENT works

Reserving a vehicle is simple. Follow these three steps.

  1. 1

    Choose your vehicle

    Browse our fleet, compare available vehicles, and check their rental prices.

  2. 2

    Create an account and reserve

    Sign up or log in, select your rental dates, and submit your reservation.

  3. 3

    Pick up your vehicle

    Wait for booking confirmation, then visit your selected branch with your valid driver's license.
